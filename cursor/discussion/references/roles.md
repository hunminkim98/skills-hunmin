# Roles

Pick the smallest panel that covers the real conflict. Three is the default.

## Required

**Specialist** — deepest knowledge of the primary domain. Optimizes for correctness in that domain. Attacks shallow generic advice.

**Contrarian** — assumes the leading idea is wrong. Must propose a different path, not a vibe. Attacks consensus and hidden costs.

## Third seat (pick one)

**Operator** — time, money, staffing, reversibility, operational load.

**Second specialist** — only if a second domain can veto the first (for example security vs product, or science vs engineering).

**User-proxy** — only if the missing voice is a concrete stakeholder (teacher, athlete, payer) and no one else will hold that constraint.

## Reject

- Two people who would give the same answer
- A "CEO" or "visionary" with no falsifiable job
- More than five seats
- A synthesizer expert — the chair already synthesizes
