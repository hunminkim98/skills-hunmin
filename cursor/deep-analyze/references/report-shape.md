# Report shape

Use this shape in chat. Do not write it to disk.

## Answer

One short paragraph or a tight list. This is the user's question, answered.

## Evidence

For each load-bearing claim:

- Claim
- Source (path and lines, URL, or command + result)
- Status: `verified` | `single-source` | `unverified`

Pull short quotes into the evidence list when the claim is a number, date, or exact wording.

## What would make this wrong

The competing hypothesis you tested, what you looked at, and whether it survived.

## Gaps

What you could not confirm, and what that does to the answer.

## Sources

A short list of the files and URLs you actually opened. No unread citations.
