# English patterns

Load this file only for Latin-majority blocks. Do not apply these rules to Hangul-majority prose.

Distilled from Wikipedia [Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) and [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) (MIT). This is a working list, not a fork of that skill.

## Content

- Inflated legacy: *pivotal moment*, *testament*, *evolving landscape*, *indelible mark*, *setting the stage*. Keep the fact. Drop the monument.
- Name-dropping: a list of outlets or follower counts with no context. Keep a citation that actually says what was said.
- Trailing *-ing* analysis: *highlighting*, *underscoring*, *fostering*, *showcasing* tacked on to make a simple fact sound deep. Cut the tack-on.
- Sales language: *nestled*, *vibrant*, *breathtaking*, *renowned*, *must-visit*. Name the thing.
- Vague sources: *experts believe*, *observers have noted*, *industry reports* with no name. Name the source or drop the claim. Do not invent one.
- Stock "challenges and outlook" closers that restate the same vague growth story.

## Words and grammar

High-frequency filler (worse in clusters): *additionally*, *crucial*, *delve*, *enduring*, *enhance*, *fostering*, *garner*, *intricate*, *landscape* (abstract), *pivotal*, *showcase*, *tapestry*, *testament*, *underscore*, *vibrant*.

- *serves as* / *boasts* / *features* → *is* / *has* when that is what you mean
- *It's not just X, it's Y* and clipped tails (*no guessing*) → say the point
- Forced groups of three that the meaning does not need
- Synonym cycling for the same noun (*protagonist* / *hero* / *central figure*)
- Fake ranges: *from the Big Bang to dark matter* when those are just a list
- Passive or missing subject when naming the actor would help

## Style

- Em dashes and en dashes: remove unless the user's sample uses them. Prefer a period, comma, or colon. Do not swap in parentheses on every cut.
- Decorative bold, bold-label lists (`**Performance:** …`), heading Title Case, heading emojis
- Curly quotes → straight quotes unless the file's existing convention is curly
- Hyphen piles (*cross-functional, data-driven, client-facing*) kept only where grammar needs the hyphen
- *At its core* / *the real question is* / *what really matters* used to inflate an ordinary point
- Announcements: *Let's dive in*, *here's what you need to know*
- A heading restated in the first sentence under it
- Fake punchline fragments, formulaic sayings, *Honestly? It depends…*
- Answering objections nobody raised; rejecting a fake alternative you invented

## Chatbot residue

Cut: *I hope this helps*, *Let me know if…*, *Great question!*, *You're absolutely right!*, *Of course!*, *Certainly!*, knowledge-cutoff disclaimers, and guesses dressed as facts (*she likely grew up…*).

## Filler

- *In order to* → *To*; *due to the fact that* → *because*; *at this point in time* → *now*
- Qualifier stacks: *could potentially possibly*
- Generic upbeat endings: *the future looks bright*, *exciting times lie ahead*

## Register

Blog or personal prose may keep uneven rhythm, mixed feelings, and *I*. Reference and technical prose stays plain. Do not add a narrator.
