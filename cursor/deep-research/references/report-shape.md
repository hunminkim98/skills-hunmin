# Report shape

Use this in chat. Do not write it to disk.

## What we know

The useful picture, in short paragraphs or a tight list. Each load-bearing sentence needs a source in the list below. This is a brief, not a 4,000-word survey and not a dump of URLs.

## Conflicts

Where opened sources disagree. What each side says, and why the split matters. If there is no split, say none — do not invent a debate.

## Uncertain

What you could not confirm, thin lanes, failed fetches, and what that does to the picture. Use `insufficient evidence` when the honest answer is that you do not know.

## What to read next

Three to five URLs worth the user's time, each with one reason. Prefer primary pages the lanes opened. Skip the ones that only repeat the brief.

## Sources actually opened

URL, kind, page date or `unknown`. No unread titles.

State the research as-of date (today) once, at the top or under What we know.
